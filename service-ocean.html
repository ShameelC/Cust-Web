Ocean Freight Services

Reliable & Cost-Effective Sea Freight Solutions

FreightCo Logistics offers comprehensive ocean freight services connecting major global ports. Through our strong partnerships with leading shipping lines, we ensure competitive rates, flexible schedules and secure cargo handling for both import and export shipments.

Full Container Load (FCL)

Our FCL services are ideal for large shipments requiring dedicated container space. We provide efficient routing, container tracking and timely delivery to ensure smooth cargo movement.

Less than Container Load (LCL)

For smaller shipments, our LCL services consolidate cargo efficiently, reducing costs while maintaining reliable transit schedules.
